Axian Animasi

Short comedy animations about everyday Indonesian news

Axian is a cartoon character who keeps misunderstanding new government policies — fuel blends, online-shop taxes, traffic operations — until his mother sets him straight. Each episode is a 40-second vertical video in Indonesian with English subtitles, published on TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.

All videos are original animations made by the channel owner. They are parody and entertainment, not official information.
